1. Clone or fork our course's [repository](https://github.com/nlp-illc/nlp1)
2. Compose your blog post under `_posts/2026/year-month-day-groupN.md` substituting your group number for `groupN`
3. If you need any assets, such as images, you can keep them under `/assets/blog/2026/groupN/`
4. When you are done, open a pull request so we can merge your post
